  - heading "How To Use" [level=2]
  - heading "Seed Map Update & Opt-Out" [level=2]
  - paragraph: The seed map has recently received a large update. If you’re having issues with it, you can go back to the old version by clicking here.
  - paragraph: This option will soon be removed, so please report any issues and discrepancies in the comments below.
  - heading "Troubleshooting" [level=2]
  - paragraph: If none of the below helps, please report in the comments and include your seed, platform and version, and coordinates.
  - heading "Common user errors" [level=3]
  - paragraph: "If your in-game world doesn’t match the map here at all, it’s most likely due to one of the following:"
  - list:
    - listitem: you entered the wrong seed or coordinates (make sure there are no leading or trailing spaces)
    - listitem: you selected the wrong edition or version (the one used to generate the region you look at)
    - listitem: you used add-ons, mods or behavior/data packs that change world generation
    - listitem: (for slime chunks) you didn’t wait long enough, since they have a very low spawn rate even in slime chunks
  - heading "Mixed version worlds" [level=3]
  - paragraph: If you started your world on an older version, you must switch between the versions in the app. Always select the version that was used to generate the chunks you’re looking at.
  - heading "Coastlines" [level=3]
  - paragraph: By default, the coastlines for versions 1.18 and above don’t match up perfectly. This is not a bug, but rather because the map shows the underlying biomes instead of the exact terrain. Near coasts, ocean and river biomes often have land, while land biomes can be underwater.
  - paragraph: You can enable the “terrain” option below the map to automatically adjust the colors of such mismatches.
  - heading "Known limitations" [level=3]
  - paragraph: Locations that don’t exist in-game
  - paragraph: "Many features are not 100% accurate, i.e., locations can be wrong or missing. These include:"
  - list:
    - listitem: Dungeons
    - listitem: World Spawn Positions
    - listitem: Amethyst Geodes (near caves, mineshafts, etc.)
    - listitem: Nether Fossils and Dried Ghasts on Bedrock Edition
  - paragraph: Various other structures such as villages and igloos can sometimes fail to generate in-game as well, but this should happen far less frequently than for the features mentioned above.
  - paragraph: On Bedrock Edition, predictions get slightly less reliable millions of blocks away from 0, 0. Some structures may be missing or sit in a nearby spot instead, and this gets more likely toward the world border.
  - paragraph: Inaccurate x/z coordinates
  - paragraph: Some features like fossils, ruined portals and trail ruins currently may only point to the center of the chunk they’re generated in. Since those structures are small, often buried, and can be offset 10-20 blocks from the chunk center, you might have to do some searching/digging to find them.
  - paragraph: Missing y coordinates
  - paragraph: The y coordinate isn’t shown for every feature. For structures that always generate on the surface or seafloor, this shouldn’t be an issue. In other cases, like mineshafts, strongholds and nether fortresses, the structures can easily be found by searching at different heights at the given x/z coordinates.
  - paragraph: Unfortunately, this leaves some structures like fossils on Bedrock and underground ruined portals in some versions. These will require some manual effort to find.

  - heading "Usage" [level=2]
  - heading "Setup" [level=3]
  - paragraph: First, use the controls above the map to enter your world seed, Minecraft version and dimension (Overworld, The Nether or The End). You can also import the seed from a savegame via “Load from save…” in the seed field’s dropdown, or use the Random button to find new seeds. Invalid seeds like words will automatically be converted to a number in the same way Minecraft does it when creating a new world.
  - paragraph: Use the expandable Features box to individually enable or disable all the supported structures and map features. Note that the available features depend on the currently selected version and dimension, and that most features will be hidden when zoomed out too far.
  - paragraph: Below the map you can further customize the view settings, navigate to a specific coordinate and expand the map on the screen. You can also generate and share deep links and screenshots. Additionally, you can use the buttons at the bottom right inside the map to zoom in and out and to rotate.

In a seed’s details you can rename or delete it and show its markers and completed locations on the map. Deleting a seed also deletes its markers and completed locations."
