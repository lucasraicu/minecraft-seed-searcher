# Stronghold coverage investigation

The current predictor produces a bounded set of predicted starts. It does not establish exhaustive stronghold coverage or physical-instance totals. No predictor/native/vendor change was made for this expansion.

`native/bridge.c:ss_scan` enumerates the Bedrock static placement regions intersecting the requested bounds, with a one-region halo for Stronghold. It keeps starts inside the requested window. Stronghold deliberately bypasses the generic structure viability rejection. It also calls `nextVillageStronghold` exactly three times and adds matching positions in the window, with the existing emission deduplication. `vendor/cubiomes-bedrock/finders.c:nextVillageStronghold` implements a village-biome/ring search; the bridge's three returned positions cannot establish exhaustive physical generation.

`src/seed_search/catalog/extract.py:extract_native` already labels stronghold coverage `partial`, method `predictor_bounded_strongholds_v2`. This is correct and remains unchanged. Positive predicted starts may be exposed; an incomplete inventory cannot certify zero strongholds or a complete density count. Summary/query completion checks must continue to preserve unknowns.

The saved actual-game evidence rejects a tempting but incorrect absence rule. For existing seed `-9103892851552028103`, exact structure membership at `(820,16,-1708)` reports `minecraft:stronghold`; the user's saved inspection confirms a stronghold there. A `/locate` query from the same X/Z reports a different position `(98,-2030)`. That disagreement does not disprove the first stronghold and must not blacklist the seed or remove the static enumeration. The full vertical-membership regression also preserves positive stronghold intervals at that column.

Reproducible evidence is in `tests/test_predictor_resolution.py`, `reviews/predictor-resolution-2026-10-20/ADJUDICATIONS.json`, and `USER_CONFIRMATION.md`. `reviews/generalization-validation-2026-10-20/COVERAGE_AND_CLAIMS.md` records 24 stronghold locate references with no matching coordinate under that comparison. This describes missing matching reference evidence, not 24 actual absences or a measured universal recall rate.

Any future coverage improvement needs its own reviewed proposal and approval. A suitable bounded investigation would freeze known physical positive columns and independent locate references, distinguish placement/start conventions, compare both current enumeration branches, and measure cost/recall on held-out saved worlds. It should preserve the confirmed static positive, retain partial coverage until completeness is established, and avoid interpreting remote locate results as exhaustive absence. No such new implementation or campaign was started here.
