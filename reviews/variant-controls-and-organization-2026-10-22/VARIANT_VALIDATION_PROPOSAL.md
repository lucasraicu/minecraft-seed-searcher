# Variant controls and activation — proposed, awaiting approval

## Objective and current evidence

Resolve the igloo-basement and treasure-bastion gates with independently observed
Bedrock controls. The completed biome release remains the baseline; its current
snapshot, policy, 222 saved worlds, original Realm observations and 2K reserves must
remain unchanged until a separately validated successor is adopted.

The activation matrix currently records zero independent variant-control worlds
for both features. Generic structure starts, a successful native return value and
Realm inventory `complete` status are predictions, not actual-game variant proof.
The methods under review are `ss_igloo_basement`, `ss_bastion_variant`, vendor
`getVariant`, and `catalog/realm_observations.py`. No predictor defect is presumed.

## Proposed bounded work

1. Inspect the saved cohort's auxiliary inventories and archived game evidence.
   Freeze a candidate matrix with seed, dimension, exact start, predicted result,
   method/build/settings identity and the independent observation to be made.
   No new seed search or landscape-wide structure scan.
   The read-only inventory found predicted positives/negatives in 214/217 worlds
   for igloo basements and 139/213 for treasure bastions; these are not controls.
   Include two ordinary worlds selected by a fixed hash independently of predicted
   variant labels; use remaining cases for targeted coverage. Freeze all cases and
   report every outcome. This bounded sample does not establish general accuracy.
2. Reuse compatible controls first. For missing controls, validate at most eight
   existing seeds in disposable isolated BDS worlds/copies, one server at a time,
   pinned edition/build/settings, two CPUs and 2 GiB memory, at most 180 seconds per
   world and 1,200 seconds cumulative game wall time. Total new output <=2 GiB.
   These are a proposed new allocation, not reuse/reset of a completed allocation.
   The first ordinary world is a canary within these same limits, not an extra run.
   Record cumulative usage durably, including startup and shutdown; terminate only
   owned servers on timeout (graceful stop, then forced stop after five seconds).
   Cleanup time counts against the total. Never restart to reset an allocation.
3. Use the existing observer/storage mechanisms. Inspect pinned template/storage
   semantics before choosing a physical variant signature. Freeze recognizers
   before looking at outcomes. Generic gold/chests/igloo presence is insufficient.
   Stay within existing per-request observer limits (including 8,192 block reads);
   at most eight bounded observation requests per world. Classify an independently
   recognized other variant as negative; an incomplete search is UNKNOWN.
   Before game runs, verify recognizers against pinned actual templates/static
   fixtures and prove dimension, API/build compatibility and spatial coverage.
   If the observer cannot classify within these limits, stop and retain the gate;
   do not infer absence from clipped volumes or silently expand the read budget.
4. Require two distinct actual positive worlds and two independently classified
   negative worlds per feature, including relevant rotation/coordinate boundaries
   where available. Record failures, unavailable chunks and inconclusive results.
   Do not cherry-pick away contradictory controls or treat timeout as absence.
5. If the current methods pass, enable only the validated public capabilities in
   a versioned registry and publish a successor from saved compatible facts. Reuse
   current summaries, projections, ledger, paging and 2K policies. Do not rewrite
   the live snapshot, original observations, Realm scores or historical receipts.
   Proposed separate derived rebuild limit: 600 seconds cumulative, 4 GiB aggregate
   RSS and 2 GiB new output INCLUDING copied history and temporary outputs. Preflight
   bytes and free disk first; preserve existing roots untouched. Reuse height/biome
   facts with no native extraction. This is explicitly separate from the BDS limit
   and the finished release allocation. Stop with a durable checkpoint on exhaustion.
   Check the aggregate staging/copy/temp size before copying; if it cannot fit the
   cap, stop rather than raising it. Each feature may activate independently only
   if its own controls pass; the other remains gated with its evidence retained.
6. If a method fails, retain the gate and produce an evidence-backed correction
   proposal. Native/vendor, personal scoring and loaded binaries stay unchanged;
   any consequential predictor replacement needs its own reviewed implementation
   scope. A gate is not solved by editing `available` alone.

## Ownership, dependencies and acceptance

Codex freezes cases, budgets, identities and acceptance, reviews every patch and
owns integration/adoption. Gemini High may own a bounded observer/recognizer/test
patch in an isolated snapshot after approval; another read-only review checks
actual evidence and disagreements. Maximum four workers, no recursive delegation.

Archive raw controls, pinned settings, observer source, logs and SHA-256 manifests.
Tests cover source/provider rejection, positive/negative/unknown classification,
parent/subtype deduplication, same-anchor rules, dimensions, retention and legacy
compatibility. Activation additionally requires actual controls, immutable-history
and personal/native preservation, query/UI checks and unchanged 10-second queries.
No automatic verification campaign, increased sweep budget or personal restart.

Relative complexity: medium validation, conditional implementation. Validation is
parallel with organization planning/index work; physical source/test moves wait
until variant implementation and its writable ownership are idle. No exact gate
activation ETA is claimed before the observations identify whether a fix is needed.
