# Actual column policy v2

Implemented policy, validated with deterministic JavaScriptCore fixtures; **not yet validated against pinned BDS 1.26.52.3 / Script API 2.10.0**. Earlier runtime evidence describes the previous extractor and cannot certify these new semantics. No server or native benchmark was launched for this change.

The extractor records `Block.isWaterlogged` only when the actual getter returns a boolean. Missing getters and exceptions are recorded as unknown, with diagnostics. `canContainLiquid` is capacity, not observed water presence, and is never used to infer water. Microsoft's [Block documentation](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/scriptapi/minecraft/server/block?view=minecraft-bedrock-stable) documents the getter and explicitly distinguishes waterlogged blocks from liquid blocks. Current official documentation is not proof of compatibility with the pinned build; feature detection is deliberately conservative.

Primary water and waterlogged vegetation contribute to the highest contiguous observed water run. Air or dry vegetation breaks its depth; disconnected lower water is not added. Depth is a count of occupied block cells, not exact fluid volume or fractional surface height. `waterSurface.blockY` is the observed upper occupied cell; `continuousHeight` remains null. Waterlogged solid obstructions record positive water presence but are not traversable open water. Scanning stops at the first observed support, so buried lakes cannot become surface water.

Thin snow is retained as cover while its supporting block supplies the support height. Solid snow, powder snow and ice stop scanning and record cover with hidden substrate. Frozen water is not classified as open liquid. Support materials are labelled terrain-material candidates or unclassified obstructions; their block identities do not prove that structures or player placement can be excluded. Every raster `natural_ground` cell remains false. Height represents observed support, including solid snow/ice cover, not a certified natural substrate elevation.

Raster geometry excludes cells with missing/failed overlay reads, invalid depth, or legacy columns that never inspected overlay water. Stable observations and successful saved-block checks remain required by the existing adapter. Each observed non-air scanned cell is a snapshot probe; available getter flags are checked against actual saved overlay palette water. Tooling identity hashes the policy, extractor, decoder and raster interpretation, preventing previous observations from being silently reused under changed semantics. Historical result files are preserved.

Remaining certification gates:

- Run bounded pinned-runtime fixtures with known positive waterlogged vegetation and solid blocks, flowing/source water, thin/solid snow, powder snow, ice, and elevated lakes. Demonstrate same-tick reference agreement, multi-pass stability, and primary/overlay saved palette agreement. Getter behavior on primary water itself also needs validation.
- Verify fractional liquid surface heights/flow states separately before reporting continuous heights or exact volumes. Current outputs certify neither.
- Establish natural-ground and structure provenance using authoritative evidence. Material allowlists and foliage filtering cannot establish it; stable Script API structure enumeration is unavailable in the pinned runtime.
- Sample the entire requested geometry at the required resolution and confirm complete stable coverage before certifying connectivity. Existing selected sparse columns are bounded evidence, not population precision/recall or full-geometry certification.

Deterministic controls exercise true waterlogged vegetation, missing/throwing getters, capacity without water, solid waterlogged blocks, disconnected water, frozen/elevated lakes, snow cover, invalid depth, conservative raster masks and unresolved provenance. They execute the same pure JavaScript file imported by the behavior pack; they do not emulate the BDS getter or saved world format.

## Controlled positive fixtures and surface biome contract

The fixture instrumentation is implemented and deterministically tested; actual positive fixture execution is a separate runtime gate. `fixtures_create` requires `disposableWorld=true`, a 32×32 spacing-1 request, and dry-air preflight for every planned cell at Y300–308. It resolves all requested permutations before the first write, then records requested and observed permutations/overlay flags for nine enclosed columns: source water, flowing water, waterlogged seagrass, waterlogged slab, thin snow, solid snow, powder snow, ice over buried water, and an elevated lake. Setup failure attempts exact rollback and records failures. These are controlled mutations, not natural generated terrain.

Create emits a batched persistent journal and initial same-tick reference/native column comparisons. `Probe.request('fixtures_create', q)` reassembles its journal; `Probe.fixture_rows(q)` reads the nine initial observations. Subsequent `fixtures_scan` requests must use distinct IDs. Fluids or cover may change after simulation resumes: retain initial and later observed states, and report a missing settled flowing-water positive honestly rather than treating its fixture label as evidence.

The launch owner saves and cleanly stops the disposable container before taking the positive snapshot. After restarting that same container, load the same region and call `Probe.restore_fixtures(q, persisted_journal)`. It restores in small journal chunks and verifies exact original dry air/permutation at every target. Any failed chunk or mismatch yields FAILED, never a complete restoration claim. Save, cleanly stop, and take a separate restoration snapshot. The existing snapshot guard still forbids copying a possibly live database. Snapshot/API probes now compare primary palette states as well as names and overlay flags; unsupported/missing state decoding is explicit unknown. State equality alone does not prove fractional fluid heights. Use restoration journal `results` to construct original-air probes for the restoration snapshot. Fixture rows carry `evidenceRole=controlled_fixture` and raster conversion rejects them.

Natural column surface-biome queries use `Dimension.getBiome` at one cell above the maximum observed support, open-water top, and snow/ice cover. Filtered vegetation/canopy is deliberately ignored; the rule is recorded. Unknown surface water prevents a query, and out-of-range Y is not clamped. Names remain actual API strings, with explicit query coordinate/status/provenance. Numeric IDs are not guessed. The optional `biome_registry` action feature-detects `BiomeTypes.getAll` through a namespace import, returning names from this runtime or an explicit UNSUPPORTED/QUERY_FAILED status; unavailable registry APIs do not break startup. See Microsoft's [Dimension API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/scriptapi/minecraft/server/dimension?view=minecraft-bedrock-stable) and [BiomeTypes API](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/minecraft/creator/scriptapi/minecraft/server/biometypes?view=minecraft-bedrock-stable).

Raster metadata retains `surface_biome_observations` (`x,z,y,name,status,source`), `surface_biome_evidence=actual_api_string_names`, `surface_biome_mapping_provenance=unmapped_names_no_native_numeric_ids`, expected/observed/missing cell counts, coverage completeness, query policy and canopy rule. Missing, unstable, wrong-height, wrong-policy or invalid-name observations cannot supply complete biome coverage. Selected sparse positives can establish observed existence at those coordinates; they cannot establish absence or complete biome fit. Full biome-fit certification additionally requires validated family mapping, every required stable sample, and the required full resolution.
