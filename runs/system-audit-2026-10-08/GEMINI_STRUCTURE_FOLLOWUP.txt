Does Adding Lower-Bit Structures Slow It Down?No, it actually speeds up the overall pipeline.
Evaluating the 8 lower-bit structures (fortress, bastion, village, ancient_city, ruined_portal, shipwreck, monument, outpost) relies entirely on raw integer math executed inside the CPU's ultra-fast L1 cache. Checking all 8 structures on a single seed takes microseconds.   Because the pipeline uses a short-circuit cascade, it evaluates the mandatory filters first. If the seed lacks a Fortress in the $640 \times 640$ Nether square, it aborts instantly without checking the other 7 structures. Adding the remaining structures for scoring adds effectively zero overhead and prevents bad seeds from ever triggering the 3D terrain density checks, which are hundreds of thousands of times slower.   3.
