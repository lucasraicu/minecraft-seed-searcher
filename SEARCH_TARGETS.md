# Query targets, distances, and areas

Updated 7 October 2026. This is the implemented spatial specification; the active verification status is in PROGRESS.md and the final evidence will be in SWEEP_READINESS.md. Distances are horizontal unless stated otherwise. Area means geometric footprint, not a count of inclusive integer-coordinate endpoints.

## Shared areas and reference points

**The current plan does not scan the entire world generated by each seed.** It uses a configurable center-search window as a starting work budget. The ±5000 default is **10,000 × 10,000**, not 5000 × 5000. The selected world's playable border is a separate, smaller square. Coverage can be expanded after preflight; exhaustive whole-world coverage is not promised.

| Region | Bounds / size | Area | Purpose |
|---|---|---:|---|
| Eligible island centers | X and Z each −5000 to +5000 around coordinate origin | 100,000,000 blocks² | Configurable initial work envelope; unrelated to natural spawn |
| Overworld discovery envelope | Center envelope plus 1504-block halo: each axis −6504 to +6504; 13,008 × 13,008 | 169,208,064 blocks² | Find islands and reuse predictions for all eligible centers; raster bounds snap outward |
| Overworld playable square | Center ±1504 on each axis; 3008 × 3008 | 9,048,064 blocks² | Final eligibility for all Overworld features |
| Core overlay | Center ±600 on each axis; 1200 × 1200 | 1,440,000 blocks² | Report where nearby features are; not an extra hard filter |
| Primary overlay | Center ±1000 on each axis; 2000 × 2000 | 4,000,000 blocks² | Report where nearby features are; not an extra hard filter |
| Nether playable square | Divided center ±496 on each axis; 992 × 992 | 984,064 blocks² | Fortress/Bastion eligibility |
| Possible Nether query envelope | Centers ±625 plus 496 halo: each axis −1121 to +1121; 2242 × 2242 | 5,026,564 blocks² | Optional reusable placement scan covering every eligible Overworld center |
| Central island | Preferred equivalent diameter 300–700 | About 70,686–384,845 blocks² | Equivalent diameter derives from connected land area; shape can vary |
| Nearby surrounding land | External land within 800 blocks of the island shoreline, clipped to playable square | Variable; measure per island | Water, high terrain, and external woodland assessments |

The island center is selected on an eight-block grid; the Nether center is exactly `(island_center_x / 8, island_center_z / 8)`. The spawn is a separate safe location. Water/mountain rays start at the selected center and measure their gaps from the outer island shoreline. Woodland distance is measured to the shoreline, not automatically to the center.

Square membership uses both coordinate offsets, not a circular radius. Maximum center-to-corner distances are approximately **848.5** for the core, **1414.2** for the primary area, **2127.0** for the Overworld border, and **701.4** for the Nether border. Starts at corners can qualify; accessible structure footprint and entrance still need inspection.

Phone-validation/benchmark scans may use smaller explicitly reported windows; those are diagnostic samples, not full search coverage. There is no separate hard distance limit for each Overworld type.

For the five secondary Overworld types, average the nearest-type contributions; known absence is zero. At full proximity each type has a maximum contribution of 0.6 overall points. Multiple structures of one type are reported but do not add score. Placement attempt, biome-viable prediction, and actual generation are separate statuses. A missing unvalidated prediction is unknown, not confirmed absence.

At 0, 600, 1000, 1504, and 2127 center distance, the Overworld distance factor is approximately **1.00, 0.55, 0.37, 0.22, and 0.12**. The 1000-block scale is a scoring parameter, not a maximum query radius. No End structures, Nether ruined portals, mansions, or other structures are targets in the current configuration.

**Aggregate external woodland:** provisional preference at 30% of nearby external **land**, full coverage credit at 60%. Its exact geometric area varies with shoreline shape and water coverage; measure it rather than substituting a fixed circle/square. The initial requirement is extensive surrounding woodland supported by matching-version Chunkbase review; actual accessible tree/log supply remains an optional game check. No individual forest biome needs a specified number of blocks.

**Island woodland:** no required fraction or minimum wood area. Initial openness score is `1 − estimated_wooded_fraction`, worth 5 points. Treeless land can still be steep or unsuitable, so buildability remains a separate check.

**Water enclosure:** land found across water within the 800-block range in 85% of directions is preferred; 90% gives full enclosure credit. Width preference is assessed separately. Use 180 directions; unknown rays remain unknown.

**Mountain enclosure:** preferred 75% directional coverage of qualifying high terrain. Snowy/bare peaks are acceptable with plentiful forest below/around them. The 50-block height proxy is provisional and can accept a plateau; verify the visual mountain ring.

No fixed biome Y coordinate is used as proof of surface forests: surface-biome samples follow the tested height estimate. Sea-level samples are only diagnostics. Exact structure Y, accessible footprints and safe approach routes remain optional real-game follow-up evidence.
